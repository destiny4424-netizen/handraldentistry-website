Common questions

Good to know before you come

Anything else? Call or WhatsApp 98869 20792.

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments help us keep waiting short, so please book online, by phone or on WhatsApp. If you are in pain, come in. We keep same-day slots for urgent cases.

Will the treatment hurt?

We numb the area properly before any procedure and check you are comfortable throughout. Most patients are surprised how easy a root canal or filling feels today.

How long do braces or aligners take?

It depends on your teeth. Many cases take between one and two years. At your consultation the doctor checks your bite and tells you the expected time and cost for your case.

Will I know the cost before treatment?

Yes. After the check-up you get a treatment plan with the cost of each step before we start anything.

What age should my child first see a dentist?

Around the time the first teeth come in, and at the latest by age 2–3. Early visits catch problems early and help children feel at ease with the dentist.

Which clinic should I go to?

Whichever is closest. All three clinics follow the same standards. Specialist treatments may be planned at the Bagalkot Main clinic; we will tell you when you book.
