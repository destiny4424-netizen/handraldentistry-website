Open charts in the TradingView app

iPhone doesn't let web pages open the TradingView app directly, so a tiny Apple Shortcut does it. Make it once (1 minute):

  1. Open the Shortcuts app → tap + (top right).
  2. Tap the name at the top → Rename → type exactly TradingView Chart.
  3. Tap Add Action → search Open App → add it → tap App → choose TradingView.
  4. Tap Done.

From then on the button copies the stock (e.g. NSE:SBIN) and opens the TradingView app. In the app tap 🔍 and Paste. The first time, iPhone asks "Open in Shortcuts?" — tap Open.

Which contract to buy

How the verdict is made

  • BUY: uptrend (price and the 50-day average above the 200-day) and price is in the buy zone near the 50-day average, or a fresh breakout to a 60-day high from a tight base.
  • BUY ON DIP: uptrend, but price is stretched. Put a limit buy in the zone shown.
  • WAIT: above the 200-day, but the 50-day has not crossed above it yet.
  • AVOID: downtrend. No long futures.
  • Score: 8 checks on daily and weekly candles (trend, slope, weekly EMA, 3-month return, weekly RSI, stretch).

Managing the trade

  • Stop-loss sits under the 50-day average or the last 20-day low, 1.5–4 ATR from entry. Act on a daily close below it.
  • Targets are 2R and 3R. At T1 book half, then trail under the 20-week EMA on weekly closes.
  • MCX gold and silver futures settle by delivery: roll to the next contract before the roll date shown, never hold into the delivery period.
  • Leverage cuts both ways: one lot of GOLD moves ₹100 per ₹1, SILVER ₹30. Keep spare margin for swings and margin hikes.
